Meet Cuisine de INNHS

Cuisine de INNHS is a student-oriented food discovery platform that introduces the meals, snacks and street foods available around Ilocos Norte National High School.

Why this website exists

Deciding where to eat should not take up your whole break. This guide gathers nearby food finds in one place so students can choose quickly, eat well and support small local food businesses.
