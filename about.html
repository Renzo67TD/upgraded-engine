About Cuisine de INNHS

Cuisine de INNHS is a student-oriented food discovery platform designed to introduce the different food choices available around Ilocos Norte National High School. It aims to make discovering nearby meals, snacks, and food establishments easier and more enjoyable for students.

Our Mission

To provide students with an accessible and informative guide to food options around INNHS by showcasing nearby food finds and relevant establishment information.

Our Vision

To become a useful digital food guide that connects students with nearby food establishments while encouraging appreciation and support for local food businesses.

Our Objectives

  • Showcase different food options around INNHS.
  • Help students discover convenient and affordable meals.
  • Provide information about nearby food establishments.
  • Promote awareness of small food businesses.
  • Create an engaging and accessible digital food directory.

Every school day deserves a good food discovery.
