PUT YOUR PHOTOS IN THIS FOLDER

Upload photos here with these exact names (lowercase, .jpg):

 - hero.jpg  (big banner on Home)
 - about.jpg  (About page photo)
 - category-meals.jpg, category-snacks.jpg, category-gallery.jpg  (Home category cards)
 - find-1.jpg, find-2.jpg, find-3.jpg  (Home: Food Finds)
 - meals-1.jpg ... meals-9.jpg  (Meals page; 1-3 also show on Home)
 - snacks-1.jpg ... snacks-9.jpg  (Snacks page; 1-3 also show on Home)
 - gallery-1.jpg ... gallery-15.jpg  (Gallery page)

Using a different name or a .png? Change the matching "photo" line in js/data.js.
Keep each photo under about 1 MB so the site loads fast.
You can delete this README.txt after you add your photos.
